01 · The problem

Most gyms don't have a content problem. They have a plan problem.

The treadmill

  1. Post more.
  2. Boost the one that did okay.
  3. Run ads to a page that doesn't convert.
  4. Try another agency.

Same numbers next month.

The plan

  1. Fix the offer and the page first.
  2. Shoot to the plan.
  3. Measure what it actually did.
  4. Test the next thing.

Every month moves a number.

Beautiful footage that sells nothing is a failure with good lighting.
