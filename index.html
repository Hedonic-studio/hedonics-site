01 · The problem

Most brands aren't short on footage. They're short on ads worth testing.

The treadmill

  1. Run the same few ads until they stop working.
  2. Brief an editor who has never seen an ad account.
  3. Get back something polished that tests nothing.
  4. Start over with the next freelancer.

Same footage, same numbers.

The plan

  1. Start from what the account says is working.
  2. Write the hook and the script before anyone edits.
  3. Cut variations that are built to be compared.
  4. Feed the results into the next batch.

Every batch builds on the last.

Beautiful footage that sells nothing is a failure with good lighting.

Where the footage comes from

Most brands already have what an ad needs: founder clips on a phone, customer videos, product b-roll, the leftovers from the last shoot. We build from that first, because production quality isn't what makes an ad convert. When something is missing, you get a shot list for exactly what to film.

05 · Process

Four steps, from raw footage to ads in the account.

  1. 01

    Send what you have

    Raw footage, your product page and the ads you're running now, with whatever you know about how they're doing.

  2. 02

    Concept and script

    We write the concepts, hooks and scripts, and a shot list for anything that still needs filming.

  3. 03

    The edit

    We cut the ads and their variations, sized for the platforms you run on.

  4. 04

    Test and repeat

    You or your agency run them. What the numbers show shapes the next batch.
