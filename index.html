01 · The problem

Most gyms aren't short on content. They're short on a plan, and it's costing them members.

The treadmill

  1. Post more and hope.
  2. Boost whatever did okay.
  3. Run ads to a page that doesn't convert.
  4. Switch agencies and start over.

Wasted ad spend, wasted hours, same numbers.

The plan

  1. Fix the offer and the landing page.
  2. Run ads that are built to be measured.
  3. Track what converts, not what gets likes.
  4. Improve, adapt and grow.

Every month builds on the last.

Beautiful footage that sells nothing is a failure with good lighting.

06 · About

One process, the whole cycle.

I'm Jake Sklar, founder of Hedonics. I've made hundreds of videos for gyms and fitness brands, grown a creator partnership past 7 million followers and worked on set for national brand campaigns.

I started Hedonics because agencies and production companies kept selling their services without standing behind the results, leaving gym owners to gamble on whether they'd see more revenue, less friction or a stronger brand. Most firms controlled their process, not the result. If the gym didn't grow, the problem was always somewhere else in the pipeline, and their part was done perfectly.

Hedonics is built the other way around. Offer, ads, landing page, lead follow-up and reporting run as one cycle, with our editors and me working from one plan, so nothing gets lost in a handoff and we answer for the result, in writing. I've also worked a gym sales floor, so we build for what happens after the lead comes in. Production is something we bring when a piece needs it, not the thing we sell.
