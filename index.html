The Playbook

One-time

The plan. You run it.

  • Diagnostics: your offer, intro pricing, real audience in your radius, the gyms near you
  • A media plan on one track. Organic: a 90-day plan with 15–20 fully built video concepts. Paid: budget, targeting and 8–10 ad concepts with a test plan.
  • Handoff call, two weeks of questions, a 30-day check-in

Who it's forOwners who want the strategy to execute in-house, or already have someone who will.

Why it's built this way

Plenty of gyms already have someone making content or running ads: a coach, a spouse, the owner at 9pm. That person isn't short on effort, they're short on direction. It's a plan, not a build. Move to a retainer within 30 days and everything you paid comes off your first month.

Base Building

Organic · 3-mo min

Your own channels, built to compound.

  • One shoot day a month at your gym
  • 12–16 short-form videos, formatted per platform
  • Four graphics or carousels, a full concept brief behind every piece
  • Posting calendar, monthly report and review call

Who it's forGyms with word of mouth already working, who need to look as good online as they are in person.

Why three months

Organic is a compounding asset, not a switch. Month one is building: voice, first shoot, baseline numbers. Months two and three are when it starts pulling. Anyone promising organic results in 30 days is selling you something.

Fast Twitch

Paid · 3-mo min

Leads, on a schedule.

  • A full shoot day a month for ad creative, or two half days
  • 6–10 ads a month with variations, full Meta campaign management
  • Lead form or landing page kept tuned
  • One clean test a month, with spend, cost per lead and consults in the report

Who it's forGyms with open capacity that need bodies through the door now.

Why one test at a time

It's the only way to know what actually moved the number. At a typical gym's budget there's enough data each month to read one test properly. Run three at once and you get three guesses. Your ad budget stays in your account, on your card. I get access, you keep control.

Step up · Full Cycle

From the ad to the signed member. I build and run your lead follow-up (instant text-back, nurture, pipeline) and test a new landing page against the current winner every month. For gyms where leads come in but don't show up.

Full Program

Organic + paid · 3-mo min

Both channels, one plan.

  • Everything in Base Building and Fast Twitch, off a single strategy
  • Two full shoot days a month, full lighting included
  • Organic winners become ads, ads that work become content
  • One report for both, a call every two weeks

Who it's forGyms serious about growth this quarter, not testing the water.

Why it costs less than the two separately

It costs me less to do. One strategy for both channels, one reporting cycle, and I only learn your gym once. That saving goes to you. Full Cycle can be added on top.

Game Day

Per event · No commitment

Competitions, grand openings, seminars, member challenges.

Full coverage and a highlight cut, turned around fast enough to post while people still care. For anyone with something happening worth filming.

How shoot days work

Shoot days run light by default: one operator, camera and audio, moving with the class. That's what gets honest, in-the-room footage. When a piece needs to look like an ad (you talking to camera, member testimonials, a promo for your intro offer) I bring the full lighting kit, and I'll tell you when it's worth it and when it isn't.
